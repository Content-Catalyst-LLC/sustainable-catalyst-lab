# Sustainable Catalyst Lab v0.83.0 — Provenance-Aware Scientific Figures

v0.83.0 advances Visualization Engine 2 to 2.10.0 and makes figure lineage a first-class governed visualization object.

## Capabilities
- Dataset, transformation, model, renderer, selection, annotation and uncertainty fingerprints.
- Explicit interaction-state fingerprints.
- Base-figure preservation and lineage fingerprints.
- Verification that detects changed figure/provenance content without silently repairing it.
- Export manifests carrying lineage and verification state.
- Compatibility with v0.82 uncertainty, v0.81 markup, v0.80 spatial, v0.79 linked views, v0.78 4D, v0.77 3D, v0.76 adaptive rendering and v0.75 data binding.

## Scientific boundaries
Lab does not infer missing sources, silently replace supplied fingerprints, hide transformations, repair broken provenance automatically, or label unverifiable lineage as verified.
