- Readiness review separating execution readiness from scientific acceptance.

## Scientific boundaries
Successful reruns or close numerical matches do not automatically confirm an original claim. Replication agreement does not prove causality or truth; replication disagreement does not automatically falsify a claim. Scientific interpretation remains researcher-governed.
