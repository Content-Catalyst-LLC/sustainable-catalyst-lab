# Institutional Research Governance & Review Federation — v0.163.0

## Purpose
Coordinate explicit institutional governance and federated human review across research programs and projects without transferring scientific authority to automation.

## Capabilities
- institutional research-node registry
- governance/review body registry and mandates
- explicit federation/review relationships
- external reviewer assignments with declared independence evidence
- human-recorded review decisions and preserved dissent
- institutional sign-off and policy attestations
- review-case lifecycle controlled by explicit human authorization
- descriptive governance command center
- immutable governance snapshots and digest-verifiable manifests

## Boundaries
No automatic reviewer selection, independence inference, ethics approval, scientific-validity judgment, dissent resolution, case approval, resource/funding allocation, execution, publication, credential sharing, or direct remote callbacks.
