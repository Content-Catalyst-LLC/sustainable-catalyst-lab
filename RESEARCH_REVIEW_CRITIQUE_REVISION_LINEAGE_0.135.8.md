# Lab v0.135.8 — Research Review, Critique & Revision Lineage

v0.135.8 adds a governed review layer after the v0.135.7 publication handoff. It represents review packages, critiques, author responses, revisions, decisions, replication references and review lineage as explicit reference-first workflow objects.

The release deliberately separates review workflow from scientific authority. Reviewer comments are not evidence, responses do not determine truth, revisions do not upgrade claim status, and review decisions do not certify scientific validity. Canonical claims, evidence, findings, rebuttals and scholarly-review objects remain explicit Platform Core handoffs.

Key capabilities: 6 review states, 8 critique types, 5 response states, 5 decision states, 5 revision kinds, 5 handoff targets, deterministic review lineage, revision diffs, replication references, and cross-product handoff plans.
