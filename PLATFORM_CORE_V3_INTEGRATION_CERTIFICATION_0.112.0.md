## Certification model

Lab constructs reference-first certification plans and records caller-declared observations. Core stores suites, product registrations, cases, runs, results, exchange/trace/reproduction checks, evidence, findings, revisions, and immutable snapshots.

The certification does not invoke specialist products, execute scientific work, infer missing evidence, infer reproducibility, resolve failures, certify scientific validity or product quality, authorize or rank products, or determine truth.
