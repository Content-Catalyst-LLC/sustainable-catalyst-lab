# Lab v0.141.6 — Neural Explainability Workspace

## Purpose
Provide a governed research-facing workspace for neural explanation results while leaving explanation computation and model execution in Workspace.

## Capabilities
- explanation study and method objects
- baseline/reference-input audit
- raw attribution and activation result normalization
- feature/token/spatial attribution matrices
- method-agreement and stability diagnostics
- local explanation comparison
- provenance audit and visual contracts
- Platform Core visual-object handoff
- deterministic snapshots, export bundles, and reproducibility packages

## Scientific boundaries
Attribution is not causation or mechanism. Saliency and attention are not evidence by themselves. Method agreement is not a reliability certification. Lab does not automatically endorse a model or certify explanation faithfulness.
