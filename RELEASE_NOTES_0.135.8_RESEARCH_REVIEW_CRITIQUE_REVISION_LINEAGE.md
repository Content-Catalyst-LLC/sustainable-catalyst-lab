# Release Notes — Lab v0.135.8

Sustainable Catalyst Lab v0.135.8 adds Research Review, Critique & Revision Lineage. Research narratives can now be reviewed through explicit critiques, responses, revisions, decisions and replication references while preserving full lineage and reference-first Platform Core/Publication/Library/Workspace handoffs.

Scientific boundaries are explicit: review is not scientific certification; reviewer comments are not evidence; revisions do not upgrade claim status; no automatic acceptance, truth determination, evidence weighting, Core submission, publication or replication occurs.
