# Sustainable Catalyst Lab v0.157.0

## Cross-Study Replication & Meta-Experiment Workspace

Lab v0.157.0 promotes independent study records, replication relationships, immutable extracted effects, and cross-study synthesis into a governed research workspace.

### Adds
- immutable study revisions and source/protocol/campaign references
- immutable extracted effect records with uncertainty and extraction provenance
- direct, conceptual, methodological, anchor, and related-study relationships
- explicit inclusion/exclusion state and harmonization metadata
- preregistration-style workspace freeze
- fixed-effect and random-effects inverse-variance synthesis
- Cochran Q, I², τ², prediction intervals, direction counts, and leave-one-out sensitivity
- explicit human replication assessments with rationale
- replication matrix and digest-verifiable synthesis manifest
- explicit draft handoff into Lab v0.155.0 batch campaigns
- additive WordPress workspace and HMAC-proxied backend routes

### Scientific boundary
This release does not automatically determine whether a replication succeeded or failed, establish causality, infer study independence, correct publication bias, establish significance, or establish scientific validity. Human scientific review remains required.

### Architecture
Platform Core remains canonical object/provenance authority. Workspace/runtime infrastructure remains execution authority. Lab v0.157.0 coordinates cross-study scientific analysis and review while retaining v0.156.0 distributed coordination, v0.155.0 campaign orchestration, v0.154.0 protocol/notebook lineage, and v0.153.0 4D workspace capability.
