# Lab v0.159.0 — Integrated Scientific Review, Validation & Publication Gate

Adds a governed human-review layer above v0.157 cross-study synthesis and v0.158 independent replication networks. It assembles review dossiers, evidence snapshots, validation checklists, findings/revision actions, reviewer sign-offs and dissent, procedural readiness evaluation, and explicit publication handoffs.

The gate may evaluate **procedural readiness** from explicit records. It does not infer scientific validity, truth, causality, replication success, publication merit, or reviewer agreement. Publication-ready status requires explicit human authorization.
