# Sustainable Catalyst Lab v0.127.0 — Scientific Time-Series Laboratory

## Purpose
A reproducible research environment for ordered temporal data, decomposition, dependence diagnostics, declared forecasting models, state-space analysis, rolling-origin evaluation, change-point/regime exploration, anomaly screening, cross-correlation, and spectral analysis.

## Core capabilities
- Explicit time/frequency audit; no silent sorting, resampling, or imputation.
- Declared-period additive decomposition.
- ACF/PACF and descriptive stationarity diagnostics.
- AR and conditional-least-squares ARIMA(p,d,q).
- Holt/Holt-Winters additive exponential smoothing.
- Local-linear-trend state-space filtering.
- Forecast horizons with model-dependent uncertainty bands.
- Rolling-origin temporal evaluation and residual/Ljung-Box diagnostics.
- Descriptive change-point/regime analysis and robust anomaly screening.
- Cross-correlation and regular-sampling spectral analysis.
- Publication figure plans, reproducible snapshots, export plans, and Platform Core reference plans.

## Scientific boundaries
Temporal predictability does not establish causality. The Laboratory does not automatically infer frequency, choose transformations or models, certify stationarity, assign change-point significance, delete anomalies, certify spectral cycles, certify forecast validity, or promote outputs into Core evidence/findings.
