# Sustainable Catalyst Lab v0.134.0 — Evidence Synthesis Intelligence II

Evidence Synthesis Intelligence II is the cross-study synthesis layer for Sustainable Catalyst Lab. It coordinates evidence from studies, datasets, models, simulations, replications, causal/spatial/time-series analyses, experiments, findings, and claim-evidence objects while preserving provenance, heterogeneity, contradiction, replication disagreement, uncertainty, and methodological context.

## Architecture

- Lab remains the scientific computation and synthesis authority.
- Platform Core remains the canonical owner of cross-product claims, evidence, findings, provenance, and lineage objects.
- v0.134 is reference-first and does not automatically mutate Core.
- Quantitative pooling reuses the governed v0.64 fixed/random-effects meta-analysis numerical core.
- v0.133 assumption and diagnostic records are consumed by reference and never used for automatic study exclusion.

## Primary capabilities

- synthesis context and protocol normalization
- governed evidence/finding/claim registries
- study-characteristics and claim-evidence matrices
- direction-agreement and contradiction analysis
- fixed-effect and random-effects aggregate-effect synthesis
- heterogeneity summaries (Q, I², tau²) without automatic judgments
- subgroup and leave-one-out synthesis
- replication agreement/disagreement preservation
- qualitative-theme registry and mixed-methods integration plans
- uncertainty synthesis and evidence-coverage gaps
- publication-bias diagnostic planning without automatic correction
- robustness and sensitivity plans
- v0.133 assumption/diagnostic bridge
- hypothesis-evidence and claim-centered synthesis
- competing-findings and narrative-synthesis plans
- researcher adjudication
- provenance aggregation and evidence-lineage graphs
- publication-grade synthesis visualization plans
- deterministic snapshots, revisions, exports, project/session bindings, handoffs, and Platform Core object plans

## Scientific boundaries

The system does not automatically score study quality, weight evidence by prestige or citation count, certify consensus, select a hypothesis, change claim status, infer causality, generalize beyond declared scope, certify scientific validity, determine truth, correct publication bias, or submit objects automatically to Platform Core.

A pooled effect is a model-dependent quantitative summary, not a truth score. Majority direction is not consensus certification. A matching replication is not proof of a claim. Heterogeneity and contradictions remain visible research objects requiring human interpretation.
