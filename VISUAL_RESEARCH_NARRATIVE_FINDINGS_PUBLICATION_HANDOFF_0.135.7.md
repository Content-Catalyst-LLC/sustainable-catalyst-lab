# Lab v0.135.7 — Visual Research Narrative, Findings & Publication Handoff

v0.135.7 converts reproducible visual-analysis sessions into governed, reference-first research narratives. Researchers can compose figures, findings, methods, evidence, uncertainty, limitations, provenance, comparisons, scenes, tables, citations and appendices while retaining links to v0.135.2–v0.135.6 analytical objects.

Finding states are explicit researcher declarations, not scientific certification. Narrative order, captions, annotations, figure selection and publication layout do not create claims, alter evidence weight, determine truth or mutate the source scientific record.

Publication handoffs target Platform Core, Publication Studio, Knowledge Library and Workspace through explicit plans. Every handoff remains reference-first and requires explicit submission.
