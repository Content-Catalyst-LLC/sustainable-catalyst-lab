# Sustainable Catalyst Lab v0.131.0 — Research Question & Hypothesis Workspace

## Purpose
Formalize research questions and hypothesis systems before or alongside analysis while preserving researcher agency, explicit assumptions, falsification boundaries, expected observations, rival explanations, preregistration state, and provenance.

## Core capabilities
- Research-question normalization and registries
- Primary, null, alternative, competing, mechanistic, descriptive, and exploratory hypotheses
- Variable operationalization and observable registries
- Explicit scope/population/time/spatial boundaries
- Assumption registers
- Expected-observation matrices
- Falsification and disconfirmation criteria
- Rival explanations and discriminating-test plans
- Immutable preregistration locks plus visible deviation records
- Evidence-link plans without automatic weighting
- Method-eligibility plans without automatic method selection
- Researcher-declared decision boundaries
- Project/session/Core reference-first bindings
- Reproducible snapshots, revisions, exports, provenance, and visual plans

## Scientific boundaries
The Workspace does not generate a winning hypothesis, determine truth, certify validity, automatically reject hypotheses, select methods, assign evidence weight, infer causality, generalize beyond declared scope, or submit automatically to Platform Core.
