# Lab v0.150.0 — Integrated Computational Research Laboratory

Milestone release integrating eight specialized computational research workspaces under one governed laboratory session.

## Integrated workspaces
- v0.142.0 Integrated Neural Research Workspace
- v0.143.0 Computational Linguistics Research Workspace
- v0.144.0 Statistical & Econometric Research Workspace
- v0.145.0 Simulation & Computational Experiment Workspace
- v0.146.0 Graph & Network Science Research Workspace
- v0.147.0 Graph Machine Learning Experiment Workspace
- v0.148.0 Multimodal Scientific Experiment Workspace
- v0.149.0 Scientific Model Validation & Benchmark Laboratory

## Architecture
Platform Core remains canonical object authority. Knowledge Library remains source/document authority. Workspace remains execution authority. Workbench remains prototype authority. Lab coordinates experiment design, analysis, interpretation, review, visual reasoning, validation, reproducibility, and publication handoff.

## Scientific boundary
Integration does not turn predictions, significance, simulation output, graph metrics, embeddings, multimodal similarity, benchmark performance, or reproducibility into evidence, causality, scientific validity, deployment readiness, or publication acceptance.
