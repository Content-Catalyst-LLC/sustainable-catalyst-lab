# Lab v0.140.0 — Scientific Research Operating System Milestone

v0.140.0 consolidates the Lab scientific lifecycle into a governed orchestration layer. It references existing research capabilities rather than replacing their source authority.

## Operating model

Question → Design → Data → Compute → Analysis → Evidence → Interpretation → Review → Reproduction → Publication → Living Research → Research Program

## Continuity

- v0.137.0 Research Change Impact & Living Analysis
- v0.138.0 Cross-Study Research Program Intelligence
- v0.139.0 Scholarly Study & Original Research Package

## Boundary

Research OS coordination does not certify scientific truth, validity, reproducibility, evidentiary weight, reviewer consensus, or publication acceptance. Phase transitions remain explicit user/workflow decisions.
