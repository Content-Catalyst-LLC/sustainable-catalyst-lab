# Carbon & Nature Intelligence v0.12.0 — Carbon MRV Method Registry

Hosted by Sustainable Catalyst Lab v0.95.0.

This release establishes a governed Lab-facing MRV method registry that operationalizes the method families already represented in the Carbon & Nature knowledge/evidence layer. It is a research-planning and documentation-readiness system, not an external protocol or verification engine.

## Registry profiles

- SOC Direct Measurement
- SOC Sampling Design
- Modeled Carbon Stock Change
- Hybrid Measurement + Modeling
- Biomass Inventory Measurement
- Wetland Multi-Gas Monitoring
- Whole-System GHG Accounting

Each profile retains method type, applicable systems, carbon pools, gases, measurement basis, required inputs, required evidence, outputs, uncertainty expectations, quality controls, Library methodology crosswalk, source references, and deterministic fingerprint.

## Boundaries

The registry does not automatically select a method, determine compliance with an external methodology, assert current program rules, verify a project, certify a result, or determine carbon-credit eligibility. External rules remain versioned source material that must be checked against an authoritative current source.
