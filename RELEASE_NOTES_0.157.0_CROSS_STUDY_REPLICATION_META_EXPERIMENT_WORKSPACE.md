# Release Notes — Lab v0.157.0

**Title:** Cross-Study Replication & Meta-Experiment Workspace

v0.157.0 adds a server-backed cross-study registry and synthesis layer. Studies now have immutable revisions; extracted effects are immutable; meta-experiment workspaces can freeze their planned study set; and included compatible effect records can be summarized with fixed/random-effects models, heterogeneity diagnostics, prediction intervals, and leave-one-out sensitivity.

The release also adds explicit human replication assessment, replication matrices, manifests with digest verification, and draft handoffs into v0.155.0 campaign orchestration.

No automatic replication-success judgment, causal inference, publication-bias correction, study-independence inference, arbitrary-code execution, automatic campaign creation, or scientific-validity determination is introduced.
