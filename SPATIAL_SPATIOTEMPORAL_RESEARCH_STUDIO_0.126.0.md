# Sustainable Catalyst Lab v0.126.0 — Spatial & Spatiotemporal Research Studio

Lab v0.126.0 adds a governed spatial research layer over the retained v0.80 spatial/geospatial/raster engine. It treats CRS, coordinate units, neighborhood rules, time semantics, raster alignment, source provenance, and analysis parameters as reproducibility-bearing inputs.

## Research capabilities

- Declared CRS normalization and CRS audit without silent reprojection.
- Reproducible k-nearest-neighbor and distance-band spatial weights.
- Global Moran's I and Local Moran descriptive association diagnostics.
- Getis-Ord Gi* score calculation without automatic significance labels.
- Nearest-neighbor summaries in declared coordinate units.
- Descriptive spatial-lag regression without causal interpretation.
- Raster zonal statistics and aligned raster-change analysis.
- Explicit spatial-join planning without automatic execution.
- Researcher-declared space-time cubes and temporal change profiles.
- Slice-wise space-time autocorrelation diagnostics.
- Trajectory/path summaries and declared-threshold hotspot persistence.
- Publication visualization plans, reproducible snapshots, export/reproduction plans, and Platform Core object/lineage plans.

## Interpretation boundaries

Spatial association does not establish causality. The Studio does not silently choose bandwidths, infer spatial joins, reproject data, repair topology, attach statistical significance labels, infer mechanisms from raster change, certify geodesic distance, certify scientific validity, or submit objects automatically to Platform Core.
