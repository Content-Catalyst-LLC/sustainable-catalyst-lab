# Sustainable Catalyst Lab v0.130.0 — Scientific Research Project Studio

## Purpose
Unify the research project experience across Lab and Platform Core without duplicating authority. The Studio coordinates references to datasets, studies, experiments, models, executions, simulations, analyses, figures, claims, evidence, manuscripts, reproduction packages, investigations, protocols, environments, workflows and sessions.

## Architectural rule
The Project Studio is a reference-first coordination layer. Source products remain authoritative for their domain objects. Project state, milestones, layouts, and relationships do not change scientific meaning or validity.

## Capabilities
- Project normalization, component registry, relationships and dependencies.
- Workstreams, milestones, project-state reports and timelines.
- Dataset/model/execution/figure/claim-evidence/manuscript/reproduction/investigation indexes.
- Cross-product bindings, research sessions and explicit handoff plans.
- Workspace composition and provenance aggregation.
- Readiness reports, immutable snapshots and explicit revision plans.
- Export, publication-package and Platform Core project-binding plans.

## Safety and scientific boundaries
No automatic source-object mutation, relationship inference, evidence weighting, claim-state changes, scientific validity certification, publication, Core submission, or truth determination.
